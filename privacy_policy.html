When you interact with our website, submit a form, request information, or communicate with us in any way, we may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Full name

In addition to the information you provide directly, we may also automatically collect certain technical information when you visit our website, including:

  • Browser type and version

This technical information is collected to help us understand how visitors use our website, improve its performance, and enhance your overall experience. It is not used to personally identify individual visitors unless combined with other information you have provided.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for legitimate business purposes related to providing and improving our services. Specifically, we may use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and service requests in a timely and professional manner
  • Schedule consultations, appointments, and follow-up communications
  • Provide information regarding our AI lead recovery and appointment booking services

  • Improve our website, services, and overall customer experience
  • Communicate regarding ongoing or potential business relationships
  • Send relevant updates, service information, or promotional content where you have consented to receive such communications

  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms

We will only use your personal information for the purposes for which it was collected, unless we reasonably consider that we need to use it for another reason that is compatible with the original purpose.

Information Sharing

Ignace Marketing does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties under any circumstances. Your personal information is not a commodity, and we will never exchange it for financial gain.

We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our business and delivering our services. These providers may include:

All third-party providers we work with are carefully selected and are required to maintain appropriate security safeguards for your information. They are only permitted to use your information to perform services on our behalf and are contractually obligated to keep it confidential.

We may also disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority, or if we believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Ignace Marketing, our clients, or others.

Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect it from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

Our security practices include limiting access to personal information to authorized personnel only, using secure communication channels where applicable, and regularly reviewing our information collection, storage, and processing practices.

However, it is important to understand that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including providing our services, maintaining business records, and complying with legal obligations. When your personal information is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it in accordance with our data retention practices.

If you request deletion of your personal information, we will make reasonable efforts to honor that request, subject to any legal or contractual obligations that require us to retain certain information.
